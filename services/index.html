Art Conservation Services in India

Each artwork has its own story, materials and challenges. Explore our ten conservation and heritage services, from painting and mural restoration to stained glass, lime mortar and museum science.

Painting Conservation & Restoration

Painting conservation and restoration for ageing canvases, Mughal miniatures and damaged painted surfaces, using natural mineral colours.

Mural Painting & Restoration

Mural painting restoration in India for temple walls, lime plasters and large-scale murals. Conservation carried out on site by Lemuriya.

Heritage & Decorative Surface Conservation

Conservation of historic gold leaf, Tanjore gemstone work, stucco plaster and decorative architectural details in culturally significant spaces.

View service →

Artwork Assessment & Condition Dossiers

Artwork condition assessment and archival condition reports for paintings and heritage surfaces, prepared to museum norms by Lemuriya.

View service →

Preventive Conservation & Maintenance

Preventive conservation guidance for artworks and heritage spaces: tropical climate buffering, microclimate framing, insect mitigation and light control.

Heritage Home Creation

Traditional heritage home creation with time-honoured woodwork, plasterwork, floors and decorative features that keep traditional architectural character.

View service →

Traditional Mural Creation

New traditional murals made with time-honoured techniques, natural pigments and lime-based surfaces, in the style of historic Indian painting traditions.

View service →

Stained Glass Restoration & Creation

Stained glass restoration and new stained glass panels: careful repair of leading and fragile glass, with designs made by traditional craftsmanship.

View service →

Traditional Mortar for Heritage Structures

Traditional lime mortar for heritage structures, prepared and applied to match the original materials so repairs stay compatible with the building.
