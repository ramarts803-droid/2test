Our Approach

How We Approach Stained Glass Restoration & Creation

Every project begins with understanding the artwork: its condition, materials, history and the details that make it unique. Our approach combines scientific craftsmanship, careful spectroscopic assessment and reverence for the original artist’s intention.

To discuss this service for your artwork or heritage site, send us photographs, the location and what you would like to achieve. Our team will advise on the condition, a suitable approach and the next steps.
