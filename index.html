About the Company

About Our Art Conservation Institute

Art is more than an object. It holds memories, reflects history and connects generations. At Lemuriya Conservation Centre for Heritage, we believe that preserving these works means protecting the stories they carry.

We are an Indian art conservation and heritage restoration institute dedicated to caring for historic paintings, sacred temple murals, colonial canvases, Tanjore gilded iconography, and heritage surfaces. Every project begins with understanding the artwork: its condition, materials, history and the details that make it unique.

Our approach combines scientific craftsmanship, careful spectroscopic assessment and reverence for the original artist's intention. Rather than simply making old works look new, we focus on preserving their authenticity, historical patina, and character.

Our Commitment
"To conserve with care, restore with integrity and ensure that meaningful works of art continue to be appreciated for centuries to come."

Services

Art Conservation and Restoration Services

Each artwork has its own story, materials and challenges. Our conservation services are tailored to the specific needs of every piece, with the aim of preserving as much of its original character as possible.

01

Painting Conservation & Restoration

From ageing canvases and Mughal miniatures to damaged painted surfaces, we carefully address deterioration while respecting the original composition, natural mineral colours and fine details.

View service details →
02

Mural Painting & Restoration

Murals and mural painting restoration are an integral part of our shared cultural heritage. We work on site to conserve wall paintings, lime plasters and large-scale murals, helping bring their true character back into view.

View service details →
03

Heritage & Decorative Surface Conservation

We care for historic gold leaf, Tanjore gemstones, stucco plaster, and decorative architectural details, preserving the sacred craftsmanship of culturally significant spaces.

View service details →
04

Artwork Assessment & Condition Dossiers

Understanding an artwork's physical state is the first step towards preserving it. We assess visible decay, test solvent sensitivities, and prepare archival condition reports conforming to museum norms.

View service details →
05

Preventive Conservation & Maintenance

Preservation does not end after restoration. We provide institutional guidance on tropical climate buffering, microclimate framing, insect mitigation, and light exposure management.

View service details →
06

Heritage Home Creation

Design and creation of traditional homes in heritage style, using time-honoured woodwork, plasterwork, floors and decorative features to keep the character of traditional architecture.

View service details →
07

Traditional Mural Creation

Creation of new traditional murals using time-honoured techniques, natural pigments and lime-based surfaces, in the style of the historic painting traditions they echo.

View service details →
08

Stained Glass Restoration & Creation

Restoration of historic stained glass panels and the creation of new pieces, from careful repair of leading and fragile glass to fresh designs made with traditional craftsmanship.

View service details →
09

Traditional Mortar for Heritage Structures

Preparation and application of traditional lime mortar for heritage structures, matched to the original materials so that repairs stay compatible with the building.

View service details →
10

Museum Science & Archives

Scientific care for museum collections and archives, including documentation, environmental monitoring, storage planning and the preservation of paper, textiles and objects.

View service details →
